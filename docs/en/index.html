FAQ / Support

Frequently asked questions

If something looks wrong or export does not behave as expected, start here.

Where is my schedule stored?

On your device. Nothing is sent to any server we operate. If you back up your device, the data may be included in that backup.

Will my schedule transfer to a new device?

Yes, if you restore from a device backup. Transfers that do not go through a backup will not carry the data over, so we recommend exporting anything important as an image first.

A public holiday looks wrong.

Holidays are calculated from the rules in Japanese law. When the law changes, we update the app. Please let us know if you spot a discrepancy.

I can't save the image to Photos.

Permission to add photos may be turned off. Go to Settings -> Apps -> Salon Calendar -> Photos and allow adding photos. You can also share the image directly to another app using the share button.

Dates are hard to read over my background photo.

In the Background tab, increase the text backing or add blur. Text color switches automatically based on photo brightness, and you can also set it manually.

Can I use exported images for commercial promotion?

Yes. The exported images are yours. If you imported your own photo as a background, please make sure you hold the rights to that photo.
