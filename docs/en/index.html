FAQ

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

The free version can create monthly schedules, edit label names, use standard templates, export three sizes, share images and save to Photos. Pro unlocks additional templates, square photo backgrounds, backgrounds, frames, light accents, tag placement and tag font adjustments.

Can I make video or GIF calendars?

The current version creates, saves and shares still calendar images. It does not edit video or export GIF/MP4 or animated calendar files.

Can I use exported images for commercial promotion?

Yes. The exported images are yours. If you imported your own photo as a background, please make sure you hold the rights to that photo.
