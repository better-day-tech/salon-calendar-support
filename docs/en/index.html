FAQ

Does the app take or manage bookings?

No. It turns the schedule you enter into a still image to save or share.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free features include schedule and label editing, 6 templates, 5 backgrounds, and export, saving, and sharing in three sizes. Photo backgrounds for portrait posts and Stories are also free. Pro adds 23 templates and 9 backgrounds, square photo backgrounds, additional frames, static light accents, motifs, and schedule mark position and font controls.

Can I make video or GIF calendars?

The current version creates, saves and shares still calendar images. It does not edit video or export GIF/MP4 or animated calendar files.

Can I use exported images for commercial promotion?

Yes, you can use them for salon announcements and promotion. Make sure you have permission to use any photos, text, or other content you import.
